## Generation Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original premium desktop app icon for MultiCamera,
a professional camera calibration application. Direction C: CALIBRATION LATTICE.
A confident minimalist optical calibration symbol made from a compact two-by-two
checkerboard of four substantial square modules. Three modules are crisp near-black,
one is vivid electric blue. The four modules are separated by a very carefully
proportioned central white cross-shaped negative space, with one tiny blue diamond
precisely at its center. The checkerboard is subtly offset at one corner to suggest
registration between two cameras, but remains orderly, precise and extremely simple.
A single delicate pair of graphite registration corner brackets, upper-left and
lower-right, framing the four-square symbol without enclosing it in a full box.
The entire mark should look like a deliberately crafted Swiss industrial identity,
not a QR code, not a Windows logo, not a generic dashboard, not a game icon.
Warm-white rounded-square application tile, lots of clean white space, centered
mark uses 58 percent of the tile area. Square 1024x1024, orthographic flat vector-like
raster artwork. Broad clear geometry, impeccable alignment and optical balance,
professional scientific tool. Strictly near-black, white and electric blue only.
No typography, no numbers, no letters, no photographs, no camera body, no lens rings,
no 3D, no embossing, no glow, no shadows, no gradients, no texture, no mockup,
no surrounding scene. Exactly one finished icon only, no alternatives on the canvas.

The returned master is 1254x1254; requested prompt dimensions are not used as proof
of output dimensions.
